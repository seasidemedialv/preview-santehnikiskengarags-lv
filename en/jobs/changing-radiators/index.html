Radiator replacement - connection centres that match the old pipes

Radiator replacement is simpler when the connections of the new radiator land exactly where the old pipes end.

The labour of taking off the old unit, hanging the new one and joining it to the pipes costs from €75 each. The radiator, valves, pipe alterations and draining water from the building system are not in that sum, and whether the pipes line up is the first thing that changes the price.

Height and the distance between connections

On panel radiators the position of the connections is tied to the height. The manufacturer Purmo states for its Compact panel radiators that the distance between connection centres is the radiator height minus 50 mm. So a Compact radiator 500 mm tall has centres 450 mm apart, and one 600 mm tall has them 550 mm apart.

Before buying, it helps to measure how far apart the old pipes stand at the wall and choose a radiator whose connections suit that. Pipes serving an old cast iron radiator may sit at a different height from those of a new panel radiator. The difference then has to be made up with adapters or by altering the pipes. This is a job of its own, outside the price from €75.

Purmo, installation and maintenance instructions for Compact and Ventil Compact panel radiators, checked on 25 November 2026. The relation may differ for another maker or model; see the data sheet of your radiator.

Plastic plugs in the connections

A new radiator has plastic plugs in its connections that protect the threads during factory painting. Purmo states that they must be taken out before fitting. It sounds obvious, but a plug deep in the thread may not be visible from outside, and a radiator connected with one left in may not warm up.

Packaging, weight and the valve side

For its x2 Profil Vmulti steel panel radiator the manufacturer Kermi advises removing the protective packaging during fitting only at the fixing and connection points, and taking it off entirely after the renovation ends. If the room is still being painted or sanded, the radiator stays protected. It may be run with the packaging on, but no higher than 50 °C and with the air openings in the packaging open.

Weight
from 25 kg Kermi requires a lifting aid or several people to lift and fit a radiator; the weight is given in the maker’s data.
Valve side
the Vmulti radiator is supplied with the valve on the right and the bleed plug on the left; the side can be changed, tightening the valve insert to 35-40 Nm.
System type
Kermi permits this radiator only in sealed heating systems; in a private house with an open system, check this before buying.

Element Average standard service life
Cast iron radiators 50 years

The table holds average values from Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 907, used when a building is surveyed. They do not mean that a radiator must be changed after 50 years or that it cannot fail before then.

Article 4, part 1 of the Apartment Property Law also counts the heating units inside a flat as common property if they depend on common property utility lines. In a block of flats a radiator change is therefore usually agreed with the building manager, unless the house documents say otherwise. How and when the system may be drained also affects the labour price.

Removing the old cast iron radiator

Taking off the old unit is part of the price from €75. A cast iron radiator is heavy, and with many sections it is not safe for one person to lift. Water must be let out of it before removal, and old joints may be rusted, so they sometimes have to be cut.

If a radiator has been painted many times, there may be paint on the joint threads too, and they are then hard to unscrew. Draining needs somewhere to run the water and a floor covering, because water in an old radiator can be dark and leave stains.

Brackets and the wall

A new radiator usually comes with its own brackets, and the old hooks usually do not suit it. Wall fixings are the third price factor: a concrete panel has to be drilled differently from brick or a timber wall. Moving a radiator to another spot is no longer a simple swap, because the pipes must be run to the new place.

Radiator, pipes and building manager

Can the new radiator come from a different maker than the old one?

Yes, if its connection centres and connection type match the old pipes. If they do not, altering the pipes is charged separately.

The new radiator stays cold after fitting: what is checked first?

First check that the plastic plugs, which Purmo says must be removed before fitting, are out of the connections. After that, look at the valves and at air in the system.

Can the radiator hang on the old hooks?

Usually not, because the new radiator has its own brackets with different spacing. The price also depends on the wall fixings.

Does the price include removing the old radiator?

Yes, from €75 covers the whole job: removing the old unit, hanging the new one and connecting it to the pipes. The radiator, valves, pipe alterations and draining water from the building system come on top.
