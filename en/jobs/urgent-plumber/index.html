Such a visit can be requested with the form, also at night and at weekends, from €40. If your home policy includes an assistance service, check what it covers, because a tradesperson sent by the policy company may do part of the work.

Home assistance in a policy: the BTA terms as an example

A home policy may come with an assistance service, under which the company that issued the policy sends a tradesperson itself. For example, under the terms of BTA’s “Palīdzība mājoklī” service, plumbing help includes stopping a leak caused by a pipe fracture, burst or blockage, and putting in a temporary solution.

Under the same terms, the assistance does not include maintenance and routine repair of pipework, or replacing or connecting equipment such as a water heater. Other companies may set different conditions, so read your own policy. Whether losses will be paid is a separate question, decided by the company that issued the policy.

If your policy has no assistance service, if it does not cover your case, or if its tradesperson cannot come when needed, you can request an urgent visit here.

Whether or not your policy has an assistance service, Article 30 of the Insurance Contract Law applies. Under it, the policy company covers reported, provable and reasonable expenses of urgent measures to prevent and limit damage, even unsuccessful ones. Unless the contract says otherwise, they may not exceed the amount of the loss. If you have a home policy, keep the invoice for the urgent visit and report the incident; whether and how much will be paid is the company’s decision.

This is one company’s example, not the content of all policies: AAS “BTA Baltic Insurance Company”, “Palīdzība mājoklī”, terms 0801-PAL2 (2 December 2024), checked 25 November 2026.

Where an urgent visit starts and where it ends

What is included
The plumber closes the necessary valve and stops the damage spreading while water is running or a drain is backing up.
What comes afterwards
The permanent repair and materials are a separate sum: resealing a joint, replacing a piece of pipe or clearing a blockage.
What changes the sum
The hour of day or night and the day of the week, access to the stop valve, and the extent of the damage.

The stop valve may sit behind tiled boxing or a toilet cabinet cover that must come off first, or the old valve may no longer close. The water then has to be stopped further back, for example at the riser valve in the cellar, and the building manager usually has to be told. The permanent repairs are described on the job pages: water leaks for a leaking joint or pipe, and drain clearing for a blocked drain.

A drain backing up on the lowest floors

A burst pipe is not the only emergency. If the shared sewer pipe in a block of flats is blocked, water used on the upper floors looks for a way out lower down. It can appear in a first or second floor bath, shower tray or toilet. Your flat then overflows even if you are not using any water yourself.

In the BTA terms mentioned above, a blockage is also one of the cases where assistance applies. An urgent visit first stops the damage spreading, while clearing the blockage or washing out the pipe is the next job with its own price. If the blockage is in the building’s shared pipe, the building manager needs to know too.

The fault is in a neighbour's flat and nobody is home

If a shared riser is leaking or water comes from the flat above and nobody answers the door, the damage usually cannot be repaired from your side. Article 10, part 1, point 7 of the Apartment Property Law places a duty on the flat owner. The owner must let specialists authorised by the community of flat owners or the manager do what is needed in the flat for the building’s pipework to function normally, and inspect the flat.

This does not mean anyone may force their way into someone else’s flat without the owner’s consent; if the owner refuses entry, the way to compel it is usually through the courts. So first inform the building manager, who can shut off the riser in the cellar, and the plumber works where the water can be stopped.

A leak before the water meter in a private house

In a house with its own supply inlet, a pipe can also burst between the street and the meter. The house valve after the meter will not stop the water, because the damage is before it. Riga’s water service rules have a requirement for this case. On finding an emergency or serious damage in the water supply or sewer system before the billing meter, the user must immediately inform the service provider, which in Riga is SIA “Rīgas ūdens”.

Reporting this is the service user’s duty whether or not a plumber has already been requested. The plumber works on the house side after the meter: if the leak is there, the water can be stopped at the house valve.

If the water has to be cut off so the damage can be repaired, point 67 of Riga binding regulations No. 17 applies. At the service user’s request, the provider must stop the water supply to the property for emergency repairs as soon as possible. The service user is whoever has the contract with the water company: usually the owner of a private house, and usually the manager of a block of flats.

Riga water supply and sewerage service rules (binding regulations No. 17): point 66 on notification, point 67 on shutting off water. Text checked 25 November 2026.

About policies and urgent visits

My policy has no assistance service. Can the cost of an urgent visit be reimbursed under the policy later?

That is decided by the company that issued the policy, under your policy’s terms. The BTA example above shows that the assistance service covers stopping a leak and a temporary solution, but not maintenance or routine repair.

My water heater started leaking at night. Will it be replaced on the same visit?

An urgent visit stops the water. Replacing the heater is a separate job with its own price, and the BTA assistance terms do not include replacing or connecting a water heater either. What a replacement involves is set out on the water heater installation page.

Why is there no single price for an urgent visit if the water is stopped with one valve?

Because the sum depends not on the number of valves but on the time it happens, whether the valve can be reached and how far the water has already spread. A boxed-in valve on a Sunday night is a different job from an open valve on a weekday.
