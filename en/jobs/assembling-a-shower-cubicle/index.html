Shower cabin installation - silicone on the outside, not the inside

In shower cabin installation it seems logical to seal the cabin on every side, but one silicone bead in the wrong place can cause the very leak you wanted to avoid.

Assembling the cabin parts in a prepared spot, connecting water and waste and sealing the joints costs from €125. The cabin itself, new pipe runs and building in a floor drain are not in this price.

Frame and tray seen from the inside

The maker Arcade states for its sliding shower doors that the cabin frame must not be sealed to the tray from the inside. If it is, water that has got into the frame profile cannot return to the tray, and a leak outside the cabin can result.

Frame profiles usually have openings at the bottom through which water drains back onto the tray. A silicone bead on the inside can block them, and the water then runs out onto the floor. Other models may have different maker’s requirements, so check the sealing points in the instructions for your cabin.

Tiles, grout and tray before the cabin

Arcade also sets out the order: tiling and grouting must be finished before assembly, and the tray must be level and sealed to the tiles. The silicone is left to cure for 24 hours before the shower is used.

Tiles and grout
finished and hardened, because the cabin profiles are screwed to the completed wall.
Tray
level, otherwise the doors may slide unevenly and water may not run to the waste.
Joint between tray and tiles
sealed before the cabin goes in, because it is hard to reach afterwards.

Assembly instructions for Arcade sliding shower doors, checked 25 November 2026. Other makers and models may have different requirements. Tiling and waterproofing are not part of the cabin installation price.

A frameless glass wall

A frameless glass shower corner needs a different wall from a cabin on a tray. For example, RAVAK requires a firm, solid, vertical wall for its Glassline corner, with adjoining walls at a right angle. When glass panels are moved, soft material has to stay under the bottom edge.

Spacers
hinges and handles are never fitted with metal directly against glass; the protective spacers and plastic washers from the set are needed.
Torque
glass joints with brackets and hinges are tightened to 4-5 Nm and checked again after adjustment.
Adjustment
on this model the door position can be changed by ± 6 mm by loosening the brackets of the fixed glass wall until the top edges of the panes are level.

RAVAK Glassline GSRV4 assembly instructions, viewed 25 November 2026. Sizes and tightening torques differ on other models.

Where leaks arise in the shower area

For example, in a Swedish water damage survey for 2018, the most common causes of bathroom and shower leaks were these:

Floor waterproofing
22% of cases.
Pipes
21% of cases.
Waterproofing connection to the floor drain
15% of cases.

These are not Latvian statistics, but all three leading causes in them lie in the floor, the pipes or at the floor drain, not in the cabin glass or doors. So before the cabin is assembled, make sure the drain connection has been made to its maker’s instructions.

Cabin on a tray or a floor drain

The price from €125 applies to a cabin whose spot is prepared and already has water and waste outlets. If the shower needs a floor drain, building it in is a separate job with no single price. It depends on the floor construction and height, where the sewer branch runs and how the waterproofing work is divided. The complexity of the cabin, the tray height and the position of the supplies also change the assembly price.

A simple corner cabin with doors on a tray and a cabin with a roof, side jets or electrics are jobs of very different size. The more parts, connections and sealed joints, the longer the assembly, and cabin complexity is the first factor that changes the price.

Cabins, silicone and drains

Can the cabin go up while the grout has not yet hardened?

Arcade states that tiling and grouting must be finished before assembly. The cabin profiles are fixed to a completed wall.

How soon after assembly can you shower?

For its doors Arcade says to let the silicone cure for 24 hours before use. The time for another silicone may differ and is given on its packaging.

Is a floor drain included in the price from €125?

No. Building in a drain is a job of its own, priced according to the floor construction, the position of the sewer branch and how the waterproofing work is divided.

What does cabin complexity mean for the price?

It is the number of parts, connections and sealed joints. A cabin with only doors and a tray is simpler than one with a roof, jets and electrics, and that changes the amount of work.
