Washing machine connection - a drain hose with no kinks and at the right height

The valve, trap, hose extensions and building a new outlet come on top. In makers' instructions the requirements for the drain hose matter as much as those for the water supply: how it runs, how high and where it ends.

The highest point of the hose

For its semi-integrated dishwasher the maker Franke states that the drain hose is fed into a pipe at least 40 mm in diameter or into a sink, without kinking it. The highest point of the hose must be lower than 1000 mm. The machine’s pump lifts water only to a certain height, and a hose placed higher may stop it emptying.

Makers' requirements for washing machines are similar but not identical, and they are written in each machine’s instructions. If the machine stands far from the drain, the distance is one of the price factors, because the hose cannot be extended without limit.

Franke technical guide for a semi-integrated dishwasher and Bosch directions on connecting a dishwasher, checked 25 November 2026. These are makers' requirements for their own appliances.

The cap in the trap spur

The maker Bosch states two things: the drain hose must not be kinked or squashed, and no cap that stops wastewater draining away may be left in the system. On a new trap the spur for the machine may be closed with a plastic cap or membrane. If it is not removed or cut out, the machine does not pump out and may show an error.

A hose squashed behind the machine as it is pushed into place does the same: the water cannot get away. So after connection the machine is given a test run, which is included in the job.

During the test run the machine takes in water and pumps it out, showing whether the inlet hose drips at the valve and whether the drain hose stays in the trap spur. The inlet hose is usually tightened at the valve by hand, because it has a seal in it.

Built-in or freestanding appliance

On a built-in dishwasher the hoses run behind the cabinet and under the sink, where there is little room. On a freestanding washing machine in a bathroom the hoses are accessible, but it needs a free valve and a place to drain. Whether an appliance is built in or freestanding changes the amount of work.

Among water damage cases collected in Sweden in 2018, kitchen leaks were most often caused by pipes (29%), the dishwasher (19%) and joints (18%). These are not Latvian statistics, but they show that dishwashers and joints are among the commoner causes of kitchen leaks.

A washing machine before its first run

A washing machine has its own steps before connection. For example, the Electrolux EW6SM504W instructions say to remove the four transit bolts with their plastic spacers and cap the holes. Keep the bolts, because they must go back in when the machine is moved. If the bolts stay in, the machine may vibrate heavily on spin.

Hoses
Electrolux requires connection with the new hoses supplied or new service centre hoses; old ones are not reused.
Position
the machine must not be installed over a floor drain opening.
Wooden floor
on one the machine is secured with a mounting plate, which is a separate accessory.

Oras also reminds users that the washing machine valve should be closed after use, and if no machine is connected to the mixer tap’s branch, the connection pipe (G1/2) is closed with a plug.

If there is no free valve or drain

The price from €35 applies to connection at a ready water branch and waste. If there is no free valve for the machine under the sink or the trap has no spur, those are separate, and building a new water or waste outlet is pipework with its own price. If the sink itself needs changing, read about it on the sink installation page.

Machine, hose and trap

The new machine does not pump out after connection. Where is the fault?

Bosch states that no cap stopping wastewater from draining may be left in the system. On a new trap the machine spur may be closed, and unless it is opened the water cannot get away. Another reason can be a squashed hose behind the machine.

With no free valve under the sink, can the machine be connected?

Yes, but first a valve has to be fitted or a water branch built. That is not in the price from €35 and is charged separately.

Does connecting a built-in dishwasher differ from a freestanding one?

The connection itself is similar, but the hoses run behind the cabinet and space is tight. Built-in or freestanding is one of the factors that change the amount of work.
