Flooded flat - wet ceiling, the report and tracing the leak

A flooded flat in a block of flats means two jobs at once: stopping the water and writing down what happened.

For the first, an urgent visit in Ķengarags can be requested from €40; the second needs the building manager and a flooding report. Both are worth starting as soon as the water appears.

Flooding report: photos at once, the manager on request

In a 2019 explanation on LV portāls, the head of the building managers' association states that, at the affected person’s request, the manager must come and record the flooding, usually within three days at most. It is advisable to photograph the spot at once: by the time the report is drawn up, ceilings and walls may have dried, and the edges of the stain are then less clear.

Useful photos are a general view of the room, a close-up of the stain, and the water on the floor or furniture while it is still there.

If the upstairs flat cannot be entered or its owner refuses to sign, the explanation advises writing this into the report. The report is then signed by two witnesses: the manager or a representative, and the owner of the flooded flat.

This is practical advice from an LV portāls explanation (2019), not a rule with a fixed deadline; your building’s management agreement may describe the procedure differently. Checked 25 November 2026.

If the manager is silent for a week

An LV portāls online consultation (2026) explains that legislation does not set how many days a building manager has to prepare a flooding report, and does not expressly require the manager to issue it. So check whether the management agreement provides for this. Suppose the flooding was reported at once, the damage arose in pipework the manager answers for, and a week passes with no inspection and no reply. That may be regarded as falling short of good management practice.

The same explanation advises sending the manager a written complaint, by e-mail with an electronic signature or by registered letter, setting a deadline, for example 7 days, for the report and a reply. Record the damage in photos and video and, if possible, bring in an independent expert or building specialist. The three-day period in the 2019 explanation is therefore practice, not a legal rule.

The plumber's work upstairs and downstairs

On an urgent visit the plumber closes the necessary valve and stops the water spreading; the permanent repair is a separate job. If the water comes from the flat above, the valves in your flat will not stop it: the water has to be shut off where the damage is.

If the place it leaks from cannot be seen, it has to be traced, and this job has no single starting price. The sum depends on which side the damp comes from, how many pipe sections need a pressure test and whether tiles or panelling must be opened.

Dry pipes upstairs, yet a wet ceiling under the bathroom

It is not always a pipe that leaks. Sub-point 17.7 of Cabinet Regulation No. 907 names, as a ground for unscheduled repair, leaks through floor slabs at bathrooms and toilets caused by damaged floor waterproofing. Water from the upstairs shower or from washing the floor can then seep through the slab even if all the plumbing there is sound.

Suppose a check upstairs finds taps, traps and joints dry, but the stain on your ceiling returns after the shower above has been used. The cause may then lie in the slab and waterproofing, not the plumbing. In that case it is a matter to take up with the building manager.

Cabinet Regulation No. 907 “Regulations on the inspection, technical maintenance, routine repair and minimum energy efficiency requirements of a residential house”, sub-point 17.7 (likumi.lv, checked 25 November 2026).

A stain that keeps growing for several days

Not all water damage shows at once. An industry association explains on LV portāls (2019) that its full extent may not be visible straight away, because water can soak through ceilings and walls for several days. So the first day’s photos are useful for comparison with what can be seen a week later. In addition, a person facing a recourse claim may ask for photos, an assessment and a calculation.

The affected person has duties too. Article 1776 of the Civil Law states that the injured party must take reasonable steps to prevent loss. If they could have taken them but did not, the person at fault may ask for the compensation to be reduced, except in cases of malicious intent. So move furniture and appliances out of the wet area at once instead of waiting for the report, but touch electrical devices only when the power there is switched off.

Civil Law, Article 1776 (likumi.lv); LV portāls explanations from 2019 and 2026. Checked 25 November 2026.

If water from your flat has reached a neighbour

It can also be the other way round: a stain has appeared on the ceiling of the neighbour below you. Under Article 1779 of the Civil Law, whoever causes loss by acting or failing to act has a duty to make it good. So first stop the water and work out where it came from: your fixture, your pipe or the building’s shared riser.

If the fault is in your hose, trap or joint, it is repaired as an ordinary leak. If the water comes from a shared riser that passes through your flat, the building manager has to be told, and the report procedure is the one described above.

After a flood

Can the plumber sign the flooding report?

The LV portāls explanation names as signatories the manager or a representative and the owner of the flooded flat. A plumber is not mentioned there; the plumber’s job is to find where it leaks from and stop the water.

The ceiling has dried out. Is a plumber still worth calling in?

Yes, if you do not know where the water came from. A dried stain does not mean the cause has been removed, and the same thing can happen again the next time water is used upstairs.

Does shutting off water at night cost more?

The sum for an urgent visit depends on whether it is day or night, the day of the week, access to the stop valve and the size of the damage. The price is from €40.
