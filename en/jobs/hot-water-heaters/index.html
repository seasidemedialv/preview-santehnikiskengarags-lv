Water heater installation and repair - anode and limescale checks by the maker

Water heater installation and repair covers three different jobs: mounting a new heater, descaling the tank and fixing it when a part stops working.

Mounting costs from €70 and cleaning with an anode check from €75. Repair has no single price, because the failed part sets it. Makers describe quite specifically when the tank needs checking and when the anode needs changing.

Anode wear and limescale in the tank

A magnesium anode is a rod inside the tank that slowly dissolves and so protects the tank walls from corrosion. For its EWH Heatronic DryHeat series the manufacturer Electrolux states that an anode worn by 30% must be replaced with a new one recommended by the maker. So the anode does not have to be fully dissolved before it is changed.

For its small GT 5-15 models the manufacturer Gorenje states that the first preventive check at an authorised service is due about 36 months after installation. Anode wear is checked and limescale removed if needed. Other manuals may set different intervals, so keep the manual of your heater and the installation date.

Cleaning from €75 includes draining the tank, washing out deposits, cleaning the element and inspecting the anode. A new anode, seal and other parts are added, and the price changes with tank size, the amount of limescale and access to the flange.

A heater left unused for long

Gorenje gives clear advice for the same models: if the heater is unused for a long time, set the thermostat to the frost protection position and leave the power on. The water in the tank is then kept at about 9 °C. If you do disconnect the power, drain the heater before frost, otherwise the water in the tank may freeze.

Sources: Gorenje GT 5-15 and Electrolux EWH Heatronic DryHeat manuals (viewed on 25 November 2026). Intervals may differ for other models; see your heater’s manual for its own.

An instantaneous heater without a tank

Not every heater is a tank. An instantaneous heater warms water as you run it, and it has requirements of its own. The installation chapter in the maker’s manual is intended for a qualified specialist. For its electronically controlled DCE-S Plus model the manufacturer Stiebel Eltron states:

After a water supply interruption
switch off the power at the fuse, bleed the heater and the cold supply pipe and only then restore power, or the heating coil may be damaged.
Air during operation
the air detector stops heating for one minute and cold water may run briefly; this is not a fault.
Taps
pressure mixer taps are needed, and open-outlet taps are not permitted on this model.
Water
its specific electrical resistance must not be lower than stated on the rating plate; the water supplier can tell you the value.

In a block of flats water is sometimes turned off during riser works, so the first requirement matters especially there. As for location, point 43 of LBN 221-15 recommends, for new and altered buildings, placing the heater at the centre of the demand, meaning close to the taps where hot water is used most.

Installation date and the rating plate

If a maker sets the first check at a given time after installation, as Gorenje does (about 36 months), you need to know that date. The easiest way is to write it on a sticker by the heater or keep the purchase receipt with the manual.

Model and volume
usually on the plate on the casing; use them to find the manual and spare parts.
Installation date
the maker’s service interval is counted from it.
Last cleaning
the date and whether the anode was changed.

These facts help to decide whether cleaning is enough or repair is needed, and are useful when describing the heater in a request.

Tank, anode and frost mode

Is the anode inspection part of descaling?

Yes, the cleaning job from €75 includes looking at the anode. If it needs replacing, the new anode is added as a part.

What does the frost position on a thermostat mean?

It is a thermostat setting for times when the heater is not used. On Gorenje GT 5-15 models it keeps the water in the tank at about 9 °C so that it does not freeze while idle. The power must stay connected during this time.

Can a heater be moved to another wall?

Yes, and it is a mounting job. What then matters is the weight of the heater, whether the new wall will hold it and the distance to the pipes; new pipes up to that point are charged separately.

Where on the heater are the model and volume shown?

They are usually on the maker’s sticker or a metal plate on the casing. If the plate cannot be read, the volume can be found from the manual or purchase documents.
