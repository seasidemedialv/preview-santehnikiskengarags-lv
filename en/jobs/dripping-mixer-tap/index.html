Mixer tap repair - a cartridge that looks the same but does not fit

In mixer tap repair two cartridges can look alike and still not fit in each other’s place.

Replacing a cartridge or seals, or putting a new tap on the existing supplies, costs from €25 per tap; bought parts and the tap itself come on top. The price changes with the tap’s construction, dirt and rust, and with whether it is in the kitchen, bathroom or shower.

What has to match on a cartridge

For example, a guide from the British retailer Screwfix warns that cartridges are not universal. The new one must match the old one in three respects:

Diameter
the width of the cartridge body that goes into the tap’s seat.
Length
from the base to the tip of the stem; if the cartridge is longer or shorter, the handle may not go on or the tap may not close.
Number of splines on the stem
the grooves the handle seats on; with a different number the handle will not hold.

So take the old cartridge out before buying a new one, not after. If the tap’s maker and model are known, a spare can be looked up by them, but even then compare it with the old one.

Single-lever taps usually have a ceramic disc cartridge that controls flow and temperature together. On two-handle taps each handle has its own cartridge or washer. Thermostatic shower taps have a separate thermostatic element, and replacing it is more involved.

Releasing pressure before the work

The maker GROHE states that before changing a cartridge you close both supplies, cold and hot, and open the tap to release the pressure. Otherwise water still under pressure in the pipe between the valve and the tap can spurt out as soon as the cartridge is unscrewed.

In a kitchen the supply valves are usually under the sink; in a bathroom they may also be at the riser or behind a panel. If one of them does not close fully, that is a further repair worth doing at the same time.

Ball valves and how often to service

The maker Oras recommends servicing mixer taps once every one to two years, with suitable tools and original spare parts; exactly how often depends on water quality and use. Oras also notes that servicing is easier if the tap connections have ball-type stop valves: each tap can then be isolated without leaving other water points dry.

Another Oras instruction concerns the spout. A device with its own shut-off valve, such as a pressure washer, must not be attached in place of the aerator: it causes water hammer that damages the tap mechanism or spout.

Dirt after larger works in the building

A second GROHE instruction concerns the time after repairs: after larger works in a building the water pipe should be flushed clear of dirt so that it does not damage the mixer tap. In a block of flats such works include, for example, replacing risers or cutting off water to the whole building, and rust and sand particles can be left in the pipes afterwards.

GROHE instructions on changing a cartridge and the Screwfix guide to tap cartridges, checked 25 November 2026. These are recommendations from a maker and a retailer, not Latvian standards.

Kitchen, bathroom or shower

A kitchen tap with a pull-out spray, a bath tap with a diverter and a thermostatic shower tap are built differently, and that changes the amount of work. A tap left untouched for many years may be rusted at the supplies, and removing it then takes more time.

If the basin is changed along with the tap, mounting it is another job with its own price; see sink installation.

Cartridge, handle and valves

Why take the old cartridge to the shop?

The Screwfix guide warns that cartridges are not universal: the new one must match in diameter, length and number of splines on the stem. Comparing is easiest with the old one in your hand.

What is meant by the number of splines on the stem?

They are the grooves on the cartridge stem that the handle seats on. If the number differs, the handle will not hold on the new cartridge or will spin freely.

Why open the tap before taking the cartridge out?

To release the pressure. GROHE says to close both supplies first and then open the tap: water left in the pipe runs out through the spout instead of spurting when the cartridge is unscrewed.

Do a tap change and a cartridge change share one price?

Yes, from €25 per tap applies both to replacing a cartridge or seals and to putting another tap on the existing supplies. Parts and the tap itself come on top.

Can a pressure washer be connected to a kitchen tap?

Oras forbids it: a device with its own shut-off valve in place of the aerator causes water hammer that can damage the tap mechanism or spout.
