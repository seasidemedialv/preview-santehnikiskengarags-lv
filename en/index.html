One riser through every floor

According to the neighbourhoods portal, housing here is distinctly high-rise, with only a small plot at Kvadrāta iela given to private houses. In both cases work starts the same way: the plumber looks at the faulty part and says what needs doing and what it would cost.

The Ķengarags estate was built between 1961 and 1971, and in a block of flats the water and waste risers run vertically through flats that sit one above another. If they have not been replaced since construction, they are as old as the building, and a leak in one flat’s riser also affects the neighbours above and below.

It is also possible to replace just one section the height of one flat, marked green in the diagram: the new pipe is joined to the old one above and below. These two joints are the most important part of the job, because the old pipe stays there. What such a section costs and what it includes is written on the riser replacement page.

Even when only one tap is being repaired, the riser’s age matters. An old valve on the branch pipe may no longer close fully. The water then has to be shut off further back, at the riser, before the work.

Bath and toilet in one room

Among the panel blocks in Ķengarags there are also Khrushchev-era sand-lime brick buildings of series 316 and 318, built in Riga in 1956–1964. In them the bathroom and toilet are combined: bath, toilet and basin are in one room.

If water appears on the floor of such a room, it may come from the toilet cistern, the bath trap or the basin hose. So when looking for the fault, every fixture has to be checked, not only the one beside the puddle.

Private houses between the Daugava and Latgales iela

According to the neighbourhoods portal, private houses in Ķengarags occupy a single small plot of 6 ha in the south-east of the neighbourhood around Kvadrāta iela, between the Daugava and Latgales iela. In a house with its own yard the plumbing does not stop indoors: there is also a supply inlet from the street, a yard sewer to a manhole and the house’s own water heater.

The yard pipe has its own first-hour price, explained on the drain page. Private and garden houses are also found in several nearby neighbourhoods; each has its own page in the section Riga’s east side.

Before you send the form

I filled in the form and closed the page. What happens next?

Once you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes on the number you entered. The page need not stay open, and the request need not be sent again; how to send a request for someone else’s flat is described on the contact page.

The form lets me mark a job as urgent. When should I?

When water is running and cannot be stopped, or a drain is backing up. Such cases are accepted, also at night and at weekends. Other jobs, such as fitting a new tap, do not need to be marked urgent.

Is the €40 inspection also the repair price?

No. That sum is for the plumber coming, looking at the faulty part and saying what needs doing and what it would cost. The repair itself and parts are not included in that €40.

My bathroom and toilet are one room. Can only the toilet be replaced?

Yes, replacing a toilet is a separate job, and the other fixtures need not be touched for it. What it includes is written on the toilet fitting page.
