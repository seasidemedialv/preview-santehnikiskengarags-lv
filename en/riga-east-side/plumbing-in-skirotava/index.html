Plumber in Šķirotava - drain fall on level ground

On flat land, a plumber in Šķirotava finds that the terrain gives a yard drain no slope; only the pipe itself and the way it was laid provide it.

If the pipe has sunk somewhere, water stops there and the blockage returns at the same spot. This page also covers a water pipe to an extension or workshop and the shared riser in a block of flats.

A dip in the yard pipe where water stops

The geography section of the neighbourhoods portal calls Šķirotava’s relief very flat: almost the whole neighbourhood lies 11–12 m above sea level. For plumbing that means one thing. The land itself does not help wastewater run away from the house, so the yard sewer has exactly the fall to the manhole that it was built with, usually with nothing to spare. If a length of pipe settles by a few centimetres over the years, on level ground that can already make a hollow inside the pipe.

Water stands in such a dip even when the rest of the pipe is empty. Sand, grease and paper can settle in it, and after a while a blockage forms right there. A familiar picture: the snake shifts the blockage and the water drains, but a few weeks later it all repeats at the same distance from the house. The reason may then be the shape of the pipe, not what goes down the drain. Clearing helps for a time and jetting washes out deposits, but relaying the settled length on a level, compacted bed can solve the problem for good.

A new gravity pipe and camera inspection

When a yard sewer is built anew or extended to another manhole, on level ground every centimetre of fall has to be planned along the whole length. The pipe needs an even sand bed so that it does not later sink at single points. Joints must be pushed fully home, because water does not run fast here and can slowly seep into the ground through a loose socket. If the pipe is connected to the city network, Riga also provides for a camera inspection after construction, which is worth knowing before the trench is backfilled, not after.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17 (15 December 2017), point 19. After construction, a gravity sewer usually requires a CCTV inspection of the pipeline, in the presence of Rīgas ūdens and at the client’s expense, and a survey. Source: likumi.lv, checked 25 November 2026.

Water to an extension or workshop

In the history section of the neighbourhoods portal, development on Krustpils iela consists of low-rise private houses and blocks of flats, as well as industrial sites and warehouses. The residential part between the station and the Riga boundary is mainly private houses. Where a garage, outbuilding or small workshop stands beside the house, water is often needed there too. The pipe then has to go from the house to the second building through the ground or exposed along a wall, and each route needs its own solution. A buried pipe to the second building and a pipe along the house front look similar on a plan but differ in the work. The first needs a trench and frost protection, the second brackets and lagging.

Separate valve
a stop valve where the pipe branches off to the second building lets you isolate and drain the extension without touching the water in the house.
Pipe on a wall
an exposed run can be seen and reached, but in an unheated room it must be drained for winter or lagged.
Brackets
different ones suit brick, block or timber walls, and they go close enough that the pipe does not sag between them.
Waste
choose the sink position in the extension by where a waste pipe with a fall can be led from it, because on a flat plot that is the hardest part.

A shared riser in a newer block of flats

Šķirotava is not only private houses. The portal’s history section mentions the 57-flat block “Rubeņkalni”, built at Gravas iela 17. In such a building the water and waste risers serve every flat in one vertical line. Replacing a section or joining a new branch usually means shutting off water to the whole line. So the work is agreed with the building manager and timed to keep neighbours without water as briefly as possible. The new section in the flat is joined to the old pipe above and below. If taps or hoses there also need changing, it pays to do so while the line is still off.

Doubts about the yard, extension and workshop

The snake went through, but water still stands in the yard manhole. What can that point to?

If water in the manhole stands higher than the bottom of the outgoing pipe, the blockage or dip is most likely further on, beyond the manhole, not between house and manhole. Then the stretch from this manhole to the next must be cleared. If water drains after cleaning but later collects in the manhole again, the pipe beyond it may have settled or collapsed, and a camera inspection can show the exact spot.

The extension needs water. Can the water pipe go in the same trench as the drain?

A shared trench saves digging, but a water pipe is usually not laid beside a drain at the same level. It is set apart or higher, so that water from a damaged drain does not reach the drinking water pipe and each pipe can later be dug up separately. If the job has a design, it sets the distances. A pipe to an unheated building must be deep enough or drainable.

A workshop or utility room needs a sink, with pipes on the wall surface. What to consider?

Workshop water carries sand, paint and oil residue, so the trap must be easy to unscrew and clean, and solvents and paints should not go down the drain. For an exposed pipe on a wall, use brackets that leave a small gap, so that it can be painted and inspected. If the room is not heated in winter, the water branch needs its own valve and a way to drain it.
