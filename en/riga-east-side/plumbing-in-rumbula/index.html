How deep the water table lies

In the geography section of the neighbourhoods portal, parts of Rumbula differ in how deep the groundwater lies. This describes the neighbourhood as a whole and is not a measurement at a particular house. It still helps to know what to expect when a waste pipe must be dug up in the yard, a new supply inlet laid or a leak traced by the foundations. The nearer the water is to the surface, the more work a trench needs and the more it matters that underground joints are fully watertight. Difficult conditions are also mentioned in the north-west, but that part is unbuilt. At a particular house the true water level is shown by the pit itself or the nearest manhole or well.

New pipe meets old: terraced houses and homesteads

In 2005 the “Šķelti” village of terraced and semi-detached houses opened in Rumbula, the neighbourhoods portal says. In a terraced house each unit usually has its own water supply and waste exit, but pipes under the ground floor are concealed and reached only through an access hatch or from a yard manhole. If a drain in one bathroom starts running slowly or damp appears at the exit, it is important to know where the branches join before anything is opened. When a tap, toilet or basin is changed, the new fixture has to be connected to pipes laid during construction, and their outlet positions and diameters do not always match the new fixture’s connections.

Among the allotments there are also a few old homesteads. In such houses the water supply and drain may have been built in stages over different decades, so steel or cast-iron pipes alternate with newer plastic ones. There may be no plan of the old runs, so before a new tap or fixture is connected, establish where the pipe comes from and whether the old length is sound at all. If a metal-to-plastic joint is fitted wrongly, drips can appear exactly there after a while. So the transition is put in an accessible place.

A house with its own wastewater system

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 66 (19 December 2018, as amended), point 6: changes in a decentralised sewerage system must usually be notified within a month at the latest to the department named there. Examples are a change of owner, alteration, a change in system type or discharge volume, and a change in the declared residents. Source: likumi.lv, checked 25 November 2026.

If a house has its own registered wastewater system, a plumbing alteration indoors can become a notifiable change too. A second bathroom, an extra shower or a washing machine can raise the volume discharged, while joining new branches to the old outlet changes what reaches the system. So review the system’s documents before such work, not after. According to the project description, the Daugava bank collector built in Ķengarags in 2008–2009 was designed with a view to connecting the wastewater of the “Rumbula” housing estate later. That is a project intention, not information on which Rumbula streets are connected.

Terraced house, homestead and an old joint

The ground floor of a terraced house is damp by the drain exit. Must the whole floor come up?

Usually not. First establish whether the damp appears only when a fixture is used or all the time. If only during use, the fault is most likely in a waste joint; if all the time, the cause may be the water pipe or ground moisture from outside. Once the search is narrowed to one branch, opening the piece of floor above it is enough. Where groundwater is shallow, damp at the foundations can also come from outside water, not a pipe. Before opening the floor, look in the yard manhole too: if it is full, water may also be standing in the house outlet pipe.

The house has two bathrooms, but only the farther one's drain clogs. Where to look?

If the nearer bathroom works normally, the blockage is in the branch between the farther bathroom and the point where the two join. The farther branch has a longer path, often more bends and less fall, so hair and soap residue settle in it more easily. Cleaning starts from the nearest access point or the trap, not from the yard manhole. If the blockage recurs, the branch’s fall may be the reason, and then relaying the length helps, rather than cleaning more often.

A metal water pipe in an old homestead must be joined to new plastic pipe. Which fitting?

Usually a transition fitting with a thread for the metal pipe on one side and a plastic connection on the other. If the thread on the old pipe has rusted, the pipe is cut back to sound metal and a new thread cut. It is better to leave the joint accessible than to brick it into a wall or bury it. If the old pipe is so thinned by rust that a thread can no longer be cut, replacing a longer length up to the next sound joint is more worthwhile.
