Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to what you type into the form on santehnikiskengarags.lv.

The data is processed by the provider of this website’s service.

What the form sends

Name, phone number, district, address, type of problem, urgency and additional information if you add it. The website name, language, time of sending and a technical number are added automatically, so that one request is not received twice.

The website collects nothing else about your visit. Cookies, visitor counters and advertising tools are not switched on here.

Why the data is needed

To contact you about the plumbing job you requested and to carry it out. Once you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. The data is not sold or used for advertising.

Who takes part in processing

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and keeps a backup copy of the request so that it is not lost if the connection fails; the copy is deleted after 90 days.
  • Activepieces receives the request, passes it to the plumber and records it in the list of requests.
  • A WhatsApp message confirming receipt may be sent to the phone number given.

These services may use the data only to process the request.

How long it is kept

Backup copy in the website’s storage: 90 days. Entry in the request list and correspondence: as long as carrying out the job and questions about it require, or until you ask for erasure.

What you can ask for

To find out what data of yours has been received, to correct it, erase it or object to processing. Send the request through the same form and write what you want in the additional information field. Complaints can be made to the Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).

Fonts and images

Font files and images are loaded from this website itself, so opening a page does not send your IP address to font or image services.

Changes to this text

If the policy changes, the new version appears on this page. Last revised 25 November 2026.
